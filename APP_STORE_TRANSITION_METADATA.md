## Description

**One culinary discovery app. Four worlds to explore.**

Let Them Eat has grown. What started as a deep dive into cake — its history, science, and
culture — is now a home for four distinct food worlds, each with its own encyclopedia, hands-on
labs, and expert-level detail:

**🍰 Cake** — Celebration, baking, pastry, and cake culture. Build a cake layer by layer in the
Assembly Lab, learn the science behind every technique, find your cake personality, and plan a
full wedding or birthday cake from flavor to structure.

**🍜 Ramen** — Japanese ramen culture, region by region. Explore the Broth, Tare, Noodle, Aroma
Oil, Ajitama, and Chashu Labs, build your own bowl from scratch, and learn the vocabulary,
etiquette, and shop-finding know-how behind a proper bowl.

**🍪 Cookies** — The dough science and traditions behind the world's cookies. Understand what
each ingredient actually does, diagnose a batch that went wrong, and explore cookie culture and
vocabulary from around the world.

**🍝 Noodles** — Global noodle traditions, dish by dish and noodle type by noodle type. Explore
the world's noodle families and the dishes built from them, region by region.

Every world shares the same thoughtful approach: real history and technique, not fluff — plus a
sommelier for drink pairings, a curated kitchen of tools worth owning, and a personal library to
track what you want to try, what you've made, and your own private notes.

Everything you've already saved is exactly where you left it. Nothing about your existing Cake
experience has changed — it's simply no longer the only world here.

- No account required, no sign-in, ever
- Your saved items and notes stay on your device
- New: switch between worlds anytime from the top of the app
- Deep, expert-level content for each world — not a shallow overview

## What's New in This Version

Let Them Eat Cake is now Let Them Eat — the same app you know, now home to three new worlds:

- 🍜 **Ramen** — regional Japanese ramen styles, a full broth/tare/noodle/aroma-oil/egg/chashu
  workshop, and ramen culture and vocabulary
- 🍪 **Cookies** — cookie dough science, traditions, and troubleshooting
- 🍝 **Noodles** — global noodle families and the dishes built from them

Switch between worlds anytime from the top of the app. Your saved cakes, personalities, and all
your existing data are untouched and still right where you left them.

## App Review Notes

This update repositions the existing "Let Them Eat Cake" app as "Let Them Eat," a single
culinary discovery platform organized into four content worlds (Cake, Ramen, Cookies, Noodles),
rather than introducing a new app. The bundle identifier, signing, and App Store listing are
unchanged from the currently-live app.

Cake is fully preserved: every existing route, feature, and user-facing flow works exactly as it
did before this update, and all previously-saved user data (stored locally on-device, no
accounts) is automatically and non-destructively migrated to the app's new unified library format
on first launch — nothing is deleted or reset.

Ramen, Cookies, and Noodles are new content worlds added to the same app shell, each reachable
from the app's home screen and from a world switcher in the top navigation. They share the app's
existing navigation, save/favorites system, and update mechanism; none of them are separate
apps, accounts, or purchases. This is an editorial expansion of one app's content, not multiple
apps bundled together.

No sign-in, no user accounts, and no new data collection have been introduced. Affiliate links
(clearly disclosed in-app) continue to work the same way they did in the previous version, now
extended to the new worlds' curated product recommendations.
